# Rossmann Store Sales — Business Insights

## Q1. Which stores have the highest and lowest average daily sales?

### Findings

Stores 817, 262, and 1114 recorded the highest average daily sales, with €21,757.48, €20,718.52, and €20,666.56, respectively.

In contrast, Stores 307, 543, and 198 recorded the lowest average daily sales, with €2,703.74, €2,790.38, and €2,900.60, respectively.

On average, the three highest-performing stores generated approximately **7.5 times more sales per operating day** than the three lowest-performing stores.

### Interpretation

The results reveal substantial differences in daily sales performance across Rossmann stores, even after accounting for differences in operating days.

However, lower daily sales do not necessarily indicate underperformance, as stores may differ in type, assortment, customer traffic, and competitive conditions.

### Further Investigation

Investigate whether these differences are associated with customer traffic, sales per customer, or store characteristics. Compare lower-performing stores with similar stores before identifying them as underperformers.

## Q2. Which stores attract the highest and lowest average number of customers per day?

### Findings
Stores 733, 262, and 1114 recorded the highest average daily customer traffic, with approximately 3,403, 3,402, and 3,201 customers per operating day, respectively.

In contrast, Stores 543, 254, and 232 recorded the lowest average daily customer traffic, with approximately 240, 259, and 304 customers per operating day, respectively.

On average, the three stores with the highest customer traffic received approximately 12.5 times more customers per operating day than the three stores with the lowest traffic.

### Interpretation
Comparing these results with Q1 reveals that higher customer traffic is associated with higher sales in several stores, but the rankings do not always match.

Stores 262 and 1114 rank among the top three in both daily sales and customer traffic. However, Store 733 has the highest customer traffic without ranking among the ten highest stores in daily sales.

This suggests that differences in sales per customer may help explain variations in store performance.

### Further Investigation
Investigate sales per customer alongside customer traffic to distinguish stores generating higher sales through greater customer volumes from those generating more revenue per customer visit

## Q3. Which stores have the highest and lowest sales per customer? 

### Findings
Stores 842, 612, and 455 recorded the highest sales per customer, generating €16.16, €16.07, and €15.91 per customer visit, respectively.

In contrast, Stores 769, 353, and 1097 recorded the lowest values, at €3.51, €3.89, and €4.03 per customer visit.

### Interpretation
Comparing these results with Q1 and Q2 reveals that high customer traffic does not necessarily translate into higher sales per customer.

Stores 733 and 769 rank among the stores with the highest daily customer traffic but also among those with the lowest sales per customer

### Further Investigation
Investigate whether differences in sales per customer are associated with Store Type, Assortment, and promotional activity, using the available store characteristics and historical sales data.

## Q4. Which stores are growing and which are showing a sustained decline in sales?

### Findings
The analysis identified stores showing three consecutive monthly increases or declines in average sales per operating day.

Stores 711, 657, and 170 recorded the largest sustained declines, with decreases of 25.84%, 24.87%, and 22.58%, respectively, over the three-month comparison period.

In contrast, Stores 732, 530, and 453 recorded the strongest sustained growth, with increases of 66.20%, 54.02%, and 48.60%, respectively.

### Interpretation
The results reveal substantial differences in recent sales trends across Rossmann stores.

Stores experiencing sustained declines may require closer monitoring, particularly if they are also performing below comparable stores.

Three consecutive monthly declines in average daily sales represent an early warning signal that warrants further investigation. While these declines do not establish a structural problem, they help identify stores requiring closer monitoring before performance deteriorates further.

### Further Investigation
Compare stores experiencing sustained declines with their peer benchmarks (Q5) and investigate whether recent changes in customer traffic or sales per customer help explain their performance (Q6 and Q8).

## Q5. Which stores are underperforming compared with similar stores of the same store type and assortment?

### Findings
The analysis identified 629 stores with average daily sales below their respective peer group averages, based on Store Type and Assortment.

Stores 543, 307, and 208 recorded the largest negative performance gaps, generating 59.15%, 58.57%, and 57.02% less in average daily sales than their respective peer groups.

Store 543 generated €2,790.38 per operating day, compared with a peer average of €6,831.50.

### Interpretation
Comparing these findings with Q1 and Q2 reveals that Stores 543 and 307 consistently rank among those with the lowest average daily sales and customer traffic.

Their performance also falls substantially below that of stores with the same Store Type and Assortment, suggesting that their low sales are not explained by these two characteristics alone.

### Further Investigation
Investigate whether the performance gaps are associated with lower customer traffic, lower sales per customer, promotional activity, or competitive conditions.

Cross-reference these findings with recent sales trends to determine whether the identified stores are experiencing further deterioration or have maintained consistently low performance.

## Q6. Which stores have experienced the largest recent declines in sales, customers, or sales per customer?

### Findings
Comparing the latest eight weeks of available data with the previous eight weeks, Stores 39, 722, and 657 recorded the largest declines in average daily sales, decreasing by 34.80%, 28.87%, and 25.37%, respectively.

Stores 39, 657, and 722 experienced the largest declines in average daily customer traffic, while Stores 971, 711, and 948 recorded the greatest reductions in sales per customer, at 11.75%, 7.96%, and 7.78%, respectively.

Store 722 appeared in all three Top 10 decline rankings, showing deterioration in sales, customer traffic, and sales per customer.

### Interpretation
The results indicate that declining customer traffic is closely associated with recent sales deterioration in several stores.

For example, Store 39 experienced a 34.80% decline in average daily sales alongside a 33.81% reduction in customer traffic.

Store 722 experienced declines in both customer traffic and sales per customer, suggesting that both components contributed to its sales deterioration.

Cross-referencing Q4 and Q6 also identified Stores 711, 657, 185, and 840 among the ten largest sustained monthly declines and the ten largest recent sales declines.

These overlapping signals make them relevant candidates for closer monitoring within the Early Warning System.

### Further Investigation
Compare the identified stores against their peer benchmarks from Q5 to determine whether they are also underperforming relative to stores with similar characteristics.

Investigate changes in customer traffic and sales per customer (Q8), and examine whether promotional activity or seasonal patterns are associated with the observed declines.

## Q7. Which stores show unusually volatile or inconsistent sales performance?

### Findings
The analysis identified 111 stores within the highest 10% of sales volatility, measured using the coefficient of variation (CV) of monthly average daily sales.

Store 105 recorded the highest volatility, with a CV of 55.31%, followed by Stores 530 and 589, at 33.31% and 32.02%, respectively

### Interpretation
The results reveal considerable differences in monthly sales stability across Rossmann stores.

However, high volatility does not necessarily indicate deteriorating performance. For example, Stores 530 and 732 also recorded strong recent sales growth in Q4, suggesting that volatility may reflect substantial upward movements rather than persistent declines.

### Further Investigation
Cross-reference highly volatile stores with recent sales trends (Q4) and recent performance deterioration (Q6) to identify stores where sales instability is accompanied by declining performance.

## Q8. Is store underperformance mainly driven by declining customer traffic or lower spending per customer?

### Findings
The analysis identified 1,070 stores with declining average daily sales over the two eight-week comparison periods.

Among the stores with the largest sales declines, reduced customer traffic was generally the main contributing component.

Store 39 recorded a 34.80% decrease in average daily sales, accompanied by a 33.81% decline in customer traffic and a 1.49% reduction in sales per customer.

Store 657 experienced a 27.54% decrease in customer traffic, despite a 2.99% increase in sales per customer.

In contrast, Store 971 experienced a larger relative decline in sales per customer (-11.75%) than in customer traffic (-5.11%).

### Interpretation
The results suggest that reduced customer traffic is the main component associated with sales deterioration in several of the stores examined.

However, lower sales per customer also contributes to declining sales in some stores, indicating that different stores may require different areas of investigation.

The widespread decline across the dataset also raises the possibility of common temporal factors affecting store performance.

### Further Investigation
Compare recent changes in sales and customer traffic against stores with the same Store Type and Assortment over the same periods.

Investigate whether seasonal patterns, promotional activity, or differences in store characteristics are associated with the observed declines.
